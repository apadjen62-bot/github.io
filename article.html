Community Comes Together for Fall Events

The Daily Badger | September 2026

As temperatures begin to cool and the leaves change color, communities across Wisconsin are preparing for another season of fall activities. Local festivals, school events, fundraisers, and neighborhood gatherings give residents opportunities to spend time together while supporting organizations close to home.

Community events can also provide a way for local groups to showcase their work. Volunteer organizations often depend on these gatherings to raise funds, recruit new volunteers, and share information about programs available to residents.

For families, these events offer something just as valuable: a chance to connect with neighbors. Whether the activity is a school fundraiser, a fall festival, a charity event, or a local sporting activity, participation can help strengthen relationships within a community.

Residents interested in getting involved can check their school, community organization, municipal, and local news websites for event calendars and volunteer opportunities. Even a small contribution of time can help a community event succeed.
